AccuPrice: AI pricing for used cars

AI productZero to one

Context

Banks, NBFCs, insurers and auction platforms need a reliable price for any used car to sanction loans, write insurance and run auctions. Buyers and sellers on CarWale wanted the same answer.

What I did

  • Built AccuPrice, an AI pricing tool trained on historical used-car data, for the BFSI industry.
  • Pushed coverage across makes, models and variants so the price was available for almost every car.
  • Launched a consumer version on CarWale for people buying or selling a car.
  • Tracked accuracy and satisfaction to keep the tool trustworthy for both audiences.

Results

90%pricing accuracy
96%variant coverage
200K+monthly users of the consumer version
+30NPS
