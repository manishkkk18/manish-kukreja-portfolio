Construction takeoff, built end to end

New product lineZero to one

Context

Construction clients needed takeoffs from their blueprints: every relevant feature marked and measured. It was a new market for a company built on outdoor-services measurements.

What I did

  • Launched the product end to end: the client-facing app, the backend and the delivery workflow.
  • Designed the workflow with our cartography team, who mark features on each client's blueprints and send them back.
  • Took it live about two months ago as a manual, service-backed product to prove demand first.
  • Now scoping an AI version to automate the marking.

Results

~$500KARR projected for the first year
