Visit Health (Policybazaar Group)2022–23

Building a retail business through partners

B2B integrationsInsurtech
Context

Visit Health, then part of the Policybazaar Group, provides insurance and wellness products to 10M+ corporate employees and policyholders. Its Retail BU had just signed its first client when I joined.

What I did
  • Joined as the first product person in the Retail BU and set its product strategy.
  • Drove PWA and SDK integrations so insurers and distributors, such as Star Health and PolicyBazaar, could offer our products in their own apps.
  • Kept every product fully compliant with IRDAI regulations.
  • Optimized conversion and engagement for 100K+ monthly users.
  • Recognized with the Round of Applause Award (FY22-23).
