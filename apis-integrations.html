APIs, a developer portal and CRM integrations

B2B platformZero to one
Context

SiteRecon's measurements could only be accessed inside its own portal. Many clients wanted the data directly in their websites and CRMs, where their teams already work.

What I did
  • As the sole PM, launched client APIs and embeddable widgets for SiteRecon's measurement data.
  • Built and launched the developer portal, where clients manage API keys, whitelist domains for widgets and raise support tickets.
  • Led the integration with Jobber, a leading US CRM for landscaping and field-service businesses, launched in October 2026.
  • Own the distribution strategy for Scout, SiteRecon's AI property-measurement product, against a $1.5M ARR target.
