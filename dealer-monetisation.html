CarTrade Tech (CarWale & OLX)2023–26

New revenue lines from dealers

MonetisationZero to one
Context

Used-car dealers were CarWale's revenue engine, but every dealer, from a small lot to a luxury showroom, got the same subscription and the same listing experience.

What I did
  • Luxury Dealer Package: a premium subscription tier for dealers selling cars like Mercedes and Ferrari. Their listings got their own design, higher ranking and luxury-specific specs, so an E-Class no longer looked like an Alto. It earned about 4X a standard package.
  • SteerEasy: launched a car-inspection service for dealerships and individual buyers, and set up WhatsApp, SMS and calling channels to sell it.
  • Ad and media products: sponsored slots, lead boosts for clearance stock and new packages, recognized with the High Flier Award (May 2024).
  • Dealer Pages: Justdial-style microsites for used-car dealers.
Results
₹3.6 CrARR from the luxury package (₹30 lakh MRR)
~₹5 CrARR from SteerEasy
₹2.5 CrARR from featured and sponsored slots
+15%dealer ARPU
100Kextra monthly sessions from Dealer Pages
