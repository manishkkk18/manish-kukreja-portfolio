CarTrade Tech (CarWale & OLX)2023–26

Faster listings, less fraud

Trust & safetyAI automation
Context

New listings on CarWale waited about 4 hours for manual approval, which delayed sellers and leads. Fraudulent listings and users were also eroding buyer trust.

What I did
  • Built an AI-based image approval system that checks listings automatically.
  • Set up the Trust & Safety charter for the marketplace.
  • Deployed an automated fraud-detection system.
  • Reworked user-reporting workflows so buyers could flag problems quickly.
  • This work was recognized with CarWale's Best Performer Award (FY24-25).
Results
