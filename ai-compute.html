SiteRecon · 2026

Cutting the cost of every AI order

AI infrastructure

Context

SiteRecon sells AI-generated property measurements and estimates to US landscaping, snow-removal and paving companies. Every customer order runs through our AI model on GPUs, so compute decides both how fast an order is delivered and how much we make on it.

What I did

  • Took ownership of GPU capacity planning and per-order margin for the AI model.
  • Forecasted order demand to size the capacity we actually needed.
  • Compared GPU options and moved our workloads from T4 to A100 GPUs.
  • Locked in a 3-year reserved contract for the baseline and set the mix of reserved and on-demand capacity.
  • Track delivery speed and margin on every order, so capacity decisions follow real demand.

Results

−55%compute costs
−75%order delivery time
